# Posy — Refund Policy

**Effective date:** July 22, 2026
**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

## 1. Spark (one-time purchases)

Spark is a single $9.99 charge that unlocks one event. Because the AI-drafted plan and event tools are delivered immediately, **Spark charges are generally non-refundable.** The one exception: if the AI plan for your Spark event fails to generate, contact us at [hello@posyplans.com](mailto:hello@posyplans.com) and we'll refund the charge in full.

## 2. Plus (subscriptions)

**Billing.** Plus is billed monthly ($11.99) or annually ($99), depending on the plan you select. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled.

**Cancellation.** You may cancel your Plus subscription at any time. Your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period; no future renewals are charged after cancellation. (For now, cancel by emailing [hello@posyplans.com](mailto:hello@posyplans.com) with the email tied to your membership — a self-serve cancel option in your Dashboard is planned but not live yet.)

**Refunds.** Because Plus provides immediate access to premium features, subscription charges are generally non-refundable. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us and we'll make it right.

## 3. Charged in error

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us first at [hello@posyplans.com](mailto:hello@posyplans.com) so we can resolve it directly — this is almost always faster than a bank chargeback.

## 4. Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback without contacting us first may result in your event access being paused while the dispute is reviewed.

## 5. How to cancel

Email [hello@posyplans.com](mailto:hello@posyplans.com) with your event's owner link or the email address tied to your Plus membership. Canceling takes effect at the end of your current billing period; you keep Plus access until then.

## 6. Contact

Questions about a charge or refund? Contact us at [hello@posyplans.com](mailto:hello@posyplans.com).
