## 1. What this covers

These SMS Terms explain how Posy uses text messaging when a host enables SMS features (like RSVP reminders and day-of updates) for an event, and what applies to anyone — host or guest — who opts in to receive texts from Posy. This is a companion to our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy); if anything here conflicts with those, the more specific SMS terms here control for SMS.

## 2. Consent is separate and optional

Opting in to SMS is its own, standalone choice — it is never bundled into a general "I agree to the Terms" checkbox, and the checkbox is never pre-checked. **Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of using Posy, planning an event, or attending an event as a guest.** You can use the Service fully without ever opting in to text messages.

- **Hosts** opt in when entering a phone number to enable SMS reminders for their own event.
- **Guests** opt in separately, if a host's event allows guests to text-confirm attendance or receive updates directly.

## 3. What you'll receive

If you opt in, you may receive automated text messages related to a specific event, such as RSVP reminders, day-of logistics updates, or a one-time subscription confirmation. Message frequency varies by event. **Message and data rates may apply** — check with your carrier for details.

## 4. How to opt out

Reply **STOP** to any message to cancel SMS at any time — this takes effect immediately. Reply **HELP** for help. You can also opt out by contacting us at ajackson59@gmail.com. Opting out of SMS does not affect your ability to use Posy or your Plus membership in any other way.

## 5. Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers may change without notice.
