## 1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the individual or entity operating Posy ("**Posy**," "**we**," "**us**") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Posy website ([posyplans.com](https://posyplans.com)) and app (the "**Service**"). It applies to hosts who plan events with Posy and to guests whose information a host enters into the Service.

*The formal legal entity name that operates Posy will be added here once it's finalized (LLC, corporation, or sole proprietorship registration is still pending).*

## 2. Information we collect

**From hosts, directly:**
- **Email address** — captured at key value moments (saving, exporting, or emailing yourself a plan). This is how we tie your Plus membership and event history to you, since Posy doesn't use traditional accounts.
- **Phone number** — only if you opt in to SMS features for RSVP reminders.
- **Event details you enter** — event name, type, date, theme, budget figures, menu, timeline, and any invitation/design choices.
- **Payment information** — if you purchase Spark or subscribe to Plus, our payment processor, Stripe, Inc., collects your card details directly. We receive only limited billing metadata (e.g., subscription status, last 4 digits, plan tier) — we do not store your full card number.

**About guests, entered by the host:**
- Names, email addresses, and/or phone numbers that a host adds for RSVP and reminder purposes. Guests do not create their own Posy account; their information is provided and controlled by the host who invited them.

**Collected automatically:**
- Standard technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and usage patterns — via Cloudflare Web Analytics, and only if you've consented to analytics through our cookie banner.
- AI processing data — when Posy generates theme, budget, menu, timeline, or invitation suggestions, the relevant event details are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate that content. See Section 4.

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- Provide the Service — create and update your event, generate your plan, calculate budgets, manage your guest list and RSVPs;
- Personalize AI suggestions — including EventDNA, which remembers preferences across your events so future suggestions better match your taste;
- Send transactional communications — RSVP confirmations, reminders, billing receipts, trial-ending notices, and service updates;
- Send marketing communications — tips, product updates, and trial nudges, only if you haven't opted out;
- Process payments and manage your Plus subscription;
- Maintain and improve the Service, including security and fraud prevention;
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use guest contact information for any purpose other than the event the host invited them to.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share information with the following categories of service providers ("**sub-processors**"), each acting on our instructions:

| Provider type | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| AI provider (Anthropic) | Generates theme, budget, menu, timeline, and invitation drafts | Event details relevant to the specific suggestion (e.g., theme, budget total, guest count) — not full guest contact lists |
| Payment processor (Stripe, Inc.) | Processes Plus subscription payments | Billing details, subscription status |
| SMS/email delivery provider (not yet selected) | Sends RSVP texts/emails and reminders | Guest name, phone/email, event details needed for the message |
| Hosting/infrastructure provider (not yet selected) | Runs and stores the Service | All data stored in the Service |
| Analytics provider (Cloudflare Web Analytics) | Understands product usage (page views, performance) | Technical/usage data, not typically guest PII |

We do not permit these providers to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing their service to us. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to you where required).

## 5. SMS and TCPA consent

If you provide a phone number and opt in to SMS reminders, you consent to receive automated text messages related to your event from Posy or our SMS provider. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of using the Service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time, or **HELP** for help. See our Terms of Service for more detail.

## 6. Data retention

We retain event and guest data for as long as your event/account is active, plus 12 months after your last activity afterward, so you can return to a past event. You can request earlier deletion at any time (Section 8). Billing records are retained as required by tax and accounting law even after deletion requests, where legally permitted.

## 7. Children's privacy

Posy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Events planned *for* a child (e.g., a birthday) are created and controlled by an adult host — the child is not a user of the Service and does not provide us information directly.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **Correct** inaccurate information;
- **Delete** your information (subject to legal retention exceptions, e.g., billing records);
- **Export** your data in a portable format;
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing, including opting out of marketing communications;
- **California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** the right to know what we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. **We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.**
- **EU/EEA/UK residents (GDPR):** the rights above, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our lawful basis for processing is generally contract performance (providing the Service you signed up for), legitimate interest (product improvement, security), and consent (marketing, SMS).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ajackson59@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (for example, 30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA), and may ask you to verify your identity first.

## 9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption of data in transit and access controls limiting who can view your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. International data transfers

Our servers and service providers are located in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your own. We take reasonable steps to apply appropriate safeguards to these transfers.

## 11. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how the Service is used, including Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not itself set cookies. Analytics is only activated if you consent through the cookie banner shown when you first visit the Service. You can review or change your choice at any time using "Manage Preferences" in that banner, or through your browser settings.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you (e.g., by email or an in-app notice) before they take effect.

## 13. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact us at ajackson59@gmail.com.
