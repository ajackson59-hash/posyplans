do not repeat the cohort, alter labels, increase the timeout or substitute an

## Implemented correction

`compactArtworkReview.ts` creates one compact full-brief review. A pool of located
observations can support multiple checks; the server restores each check's exact
source ID and quote. Every substantive requirement, exclusion, count, surface and
medium remains covered. Identity comparisons retain four independently observed
features. Clear incompatibility does not require naming the incorrect character;
insufficient detail and unfamiliar positive identities remain unresolved.

`reviewDetailViews.ts` makes a fixed overlapping grid when the source exceeds 768
pixels on an edge. It copies native decoded RGB samples with no enhancement or
resampling, preserves the original candidate bytes, covers the entire image, and
records region/source/output hashes. The existing decoder composites transparency
on white. View selection uses no subject, expected answer or prior failure label.
Detail visibility does not establish prominence at the customer's displayed size.

The KPop controls receive the three retained Netflix animated character panels.
Photographic adult panels are excluded from these reference inputs. The source
article labels the fictional targets; there is no face identification of the
photographed adults. The crop, source bytes, decoded RGB and provenance are frozen.
This differs from the earlier failed joint comparison through native character
panels, complete candidate detail views and compact source-bound observations.
Its effectiveness remains an empirical question. Same-source positive reference
controls are recognition regressions, not independent likeness validation.

The unchanged v2 craft packets reuse eight exact receipts from the closed run.
Their hashes, owner, source deployment, complete stop reason, known usage and
retained raw response must all match. No previous fidelity result becomes a new
approval. The Zoey source adds one new craft call and two identity control briefs.
